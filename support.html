Support – Bible Bison

Last Updated: May 19, 2026

1. Welcome

Thank you for using Bible Bison. Our goal is to provide a safe, simple, and encouraging place for users to discuss the Verse of the Day with others around the world.

2. Need Help?

If you experience technical issues, bugs, crashes, matchmaking problems, or have questions about the app, feel free to contact support.

Email Support:
biblebisonapp@gmail.com

3. Common Issues

Matchmaking not working?

  • Check your internet connection
  • Restart the app
  • Make sure you are using the latest version

Messages not sending?

  • Verify your connection is stable
  • Try reconnecting to a new discussion

App crashing or freezing?

  • Close and reopen the app
  • Restart your device if necessary
  • Install any available app updates

4. Reporting Users

Bible Bison includes built-in reporting and blocking tools to help maintain a respectful environment. Users who violate community guidelines may be restricted or permanently removed from the platform.

5. Privacy & Safety

We do not require real names or phone numbers to use the app.

For more information about how data is handled, please review the Privacy Policy .

6. Feature Requests & Feedback
