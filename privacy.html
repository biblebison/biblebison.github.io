Privacy Policy – Bible Bison

Last Updated: April 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Bible Bison respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights when using the app.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to operate the app:

  • Messages you send within the app
  • Reports or blocks you submit
  • Basic technical data (device type, performance data)
  • A randomly generated anonymous user ID used to identify sessions and enable features such as chat and matchmaking

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do NOT collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Contacts
  • Precise location data
  • Payment or financial information

4. Anonymous Usage

Bible Bison uses a randomly generated anonymous user ID instead of traditional accounts. This ID is not linked to your real-world identity and is used only to enable app functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services such as Supabase to store and manage app data, including messages, reports, blocks, and anonymous user identifiers. These services process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies.

6. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the app
  • Enable real-time chat and matchmaking
  • Improve user experience
  • Monitor and enforce community guidelines
  • Respond to reports and prevent abuse

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or share your personal data. We only share data:

  • When required by law
  • To protect user safety
  • To enforce Terms of Service or app policies

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain messages and reports only as long as necessary for moderation, safety, and app functionality. Users may request deletion of their data at any time, and we will delete associated data where technically feasible.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, delete, or request information about your stored data.

11. Children's Privacy

Bible Bison is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under this age.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
