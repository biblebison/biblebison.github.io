Privacy Policy – Bible Bison

Last Updated: April 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Bible Bison respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights when using the app.

2. Information We Collect

We aim to collect as little personal information as possible.

  • Messages you send within the app
  • Reports or blocks you submit
  • Basic technical data (such as device type or app performance data)

We do not require account creation or personal identity information.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the app
  • Improve user experience
  • Monitor and enforce community guidelines
  • Respond to reports and prevent abuse

4. Anonymous Usage

Bible Bison is designed for anonymous use. We do not publicly associate messages with personal identities.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • When required by law
  • To protect the safety of users
  • To enforce our Terms or EULA

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We may retain messages and reports for moderation, safety, and improvement of the app. Data may be deleted periodically or upon request when possible.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Request deletion of your data
  • Request information about stored data

Contact us through the app for such requests when available.

9. Children's Privacy

Bible Bison is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the app when available.

By using Bible Bison, you acknowledge that you have read and understand this Privacy Policy.
