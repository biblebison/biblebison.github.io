Privacy Policy – Bible Bison

Last Updated: May 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Bible Bison respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights when using the app.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum amount of data necessary to operate the app.

  • Messages you send within the app
  • Reports or blocks you submit
  • Anonymous user IDs
  • Basic technical data (device type, app performance, crash data)

We do NOT require:

  • Real name
  • Email address (unless you contact us)
  • Phone number
  • Contacts
  • Precise location
  • Payment or billing information

3. Anonymous User IDs

Bible Bison assigns each user a random anonymous ID. This ID is used only for matchmaking and moderation and is not linked to your real identity.

4. How We Use Information

We use collected data to:

  • Operate and maintain the app
  • Match users in real-time chat sessions
  • Improve performance and user experience
  • Enforce community guidelines
  • Prevent spam, abuse, and harmful behavior

5. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services such as Supabase to store and manage app data, including messages, reports, blocks, and anonymous user identifiers. These services process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies.

6. Anonymous Usage

The app is designed for anonymous communication. Users are not publicly identified at any time.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal data.

We only share information if:

  • Required by law
  • Necessary to protect user safety
  • Required to enforce our Terms or EULA

8. Data Security

We use reasonable security measures to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure.

9. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain data only as long as necessary for safety and app functionality. Users may request full deletion of their data at any time. Some moderation or abuse-related data may be retained temporarily for safety reasons.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data

11. Children's Privacy

Bible Bison is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated version.
